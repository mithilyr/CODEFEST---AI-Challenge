## Next piece of work

`src/ui/` — but there's a contract gap you should decide on before I write a line of it.

**`Answer` has no natural-language answer field.** `values` is a tuple of entity names. For `1b_009` the answer key's expected answer is a *sentence* ("He is a recorded member of The Iron-Ring Cartel, which the Purge of Blackport's own article names as victor — though the Cartel's own article disputes that it won"), and the engine returns `values=('Halvard Crowhurst',)` plus `status=CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`. Every fact in that sentence is present in the `Answer` object; nothing composes them. So your "final answer" surface either (a) renders the structured fields as-is and lets the evidence panel carry the meaning, or (b) does string composition in the UI — which is formatting, not reasoning, but it's the boundary you drew and I'd rather you draw it than me.

Everything else the nine surfaces need is already in the contract: `status`, `template`, `paths[].edges[]` (`document`, `field_name`, `row`, `raw`, `tier`, `template`, `year`, `qualifier`, `inferred_inverse`), `documents`, `conflict.status` / `conflict.denial.quote`, and `note`. Source tier is per-edge, not per-answer, which is the correct granularity for `1b_009` — both sides are tier 2 and that's the whole point.

**No UI dependency is pinned.** My recommendation: stdlib `http.server` + hand-written HTML, ~150 lines, zero new dependencies, trivially defensible under the "any teammate must explain any part" rule. Streamlit would be one dependency whose rerun/session-state semantics a teammate would have to defend for no measurable gain over a form POST. Say the word if you'd rather have Streamlit and I'll take the other side properly.

## What it says now

```
The Bleeding Crown. Gravemaw Wyrm has its lair in Marrowwell Abbey,

```

## My earlier scoping was wrong on its own terms

In ADR-040 I left the links out because they "hit the ~11ms deterministic path." That reasoning doesn't hold — a dev-question link fires **exactly the same `GET /ask?q=...`** the form fires, and under `--with-model` on an uncached question it waits on the same OpenRouter round-trip. They're not a faster path; they're the same path with the typing already done.

So the busy state is now keyed on **what the click does**, not which control was used: any left-click on `a[href^="/ask?q="]`. One rule covers all three cases — the 7 dev questions, the 118 `Who is X?` links, and the spelling suggestion on a typo decline. That last one matters most: it's what a judge clicks right after mistyping a name, and a dead-feeling page reads worst there.

## Two details worth knowing

**The clicked link marks itself.** Seven dev questions look alike; a spinner on the Ask button alone doesn't say which one is being answered.

**Modified clicks are excluded.** Ctrl/cmd/shift-click opens a new tab and leaves the page where it is — raising a busy state there would leave the page permanently claiming to search for something it isn't, with no event left to clear it. `pageshow` also clears the pending link, same back-button reason as before.

There's a test asserting every `/ask` link on the front page matches the handler's selector (7 + 118, none written any other way), so a change to `_e_url` can't silently orphan the handler.
